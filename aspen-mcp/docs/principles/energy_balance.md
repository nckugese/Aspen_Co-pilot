## zh
idea: 沒有相變化時，加熱或冷卻一股物流的熱量等於質量流量、比熱與溫差的乘積。有汽化或冷凝時還要加上潛熱，這部分常比顯熱大得多。加熱器與換熱器的負荷都是這筆帳。
eq: Q = ṁ·c_p·ΔT
sym: Q 熱負荷 · ṁ 質量流量 · c_p 比熱 · ΔT 出口減入口溫度
## en
idea: With no phase change, the heat to warm or cool a stream is its mass flow times its heat capacity times its temperature change. Boiling or condensing adds latent heat, often far larger than the sensible part. Every heater and exchanger duty is this sum.
eq: Q = ṁ·c_p·ΔT
sym: Q duty · ṁ mass flow · c_p specific heat capacity · ΔT outlet minus inlet temperature
