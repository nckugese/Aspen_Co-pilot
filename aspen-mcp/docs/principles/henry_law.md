## zh
idea: 稀薄溶解的氣體，在液相的濃度與它在氣相的分壓成正比。H 越小越容易溶解。溫度升高時 H 通常變大，氣體較難溶，所以吸收多在低溫、高壓下進行。只適用於稀溶液。
eq: p_i = H_i·x_i
sym: p 溶質分壓 · x 溶質的液相莫耳分率 · H 亨利常數（隨溫度改變）
## en
idea: For a dilute dissolved gas, its concentration in the liquid is proportional to its partial pressure above it. A smaller H means more soluble. H usually rises with temperature, so absorbers run cool and under pressure. It holds for dilute solutions only.
eq: p_i = H_i·x_i
sym: p solute partial pressure · x solute mole fraction in the liquid · H Henry's constant (varies with temperature)
