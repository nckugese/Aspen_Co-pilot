## zh
idea: 每一塊理論板讓上升的汽與下降的液接近平衡一次，板數越多，產品越純。全迴流時所需板數最少；實際設計常取最少板數的兩倍左右，並隨迴流比而變。實際塔盤數還要除以板效率。
eq: N_min = ln[(x_LD/x_HD)·(x_HB/x_LB)] / ln α
sym: N_min 最少理論板數 · x 莫耳分率（L、H 輕、重關鍵成分；D 塔頂、B 塔底）· α 相對揮發度
## en
idea: Each theoretical stage brings rising vapour and falling liquid to equilibrium once; more stages give purer products. Total reflux needs the fewest; designs often use about twice that, depending on reflux. Real trays are theoretical stages over tray efficiency.
eq: N_min = ln[(x_LD/x_HD)·(x_HB/x_LB)] / ln α
sym: N_min minimum stages · x mole fraction (L, H light and heavy key; D top, B bottom) · α relative volatility
