## zh
idea: 轉化率是關鍵反應物被消耗的比例；選擇率是消耗掉的部分有多少變成想要的產物。兩者常互相拉扯：升溫或延長滯留時間能提高轉化率（不超過平衡），副反應也可能跟著變多。產率是兩者的乘積。
eq: Y = X·S
sym: Y 產率 · X 關鍵反應物轉化率 · S 轉化掉的反應物變成產物的比例
## en
idea: Conversion is the share of the key reactant consumed; selectivity is the share of that which became the wanted product. They often pull apart: more heat or residence time converts more, up to equilibrium, and may feed side reactions too. Yield is X times S.
eq: Y = X·S
sym: Y yield · X key reactant conversion · S share of the converted reactant that becomes product
