## zh
idea: 進料在指定的溫度與壓力下分成互相平衡的汽相與液相。兩相的量像槓桿：進料組成落在液相與汽相組成之間，離哪一相越近，那一相就越多。升溫或降壓會讓汽化變多。
eq: V/F = (z − x) / (y − x)
sym: V/F 汽化分率 · z、x、y 同一成分在進料、液相、汽相的莫耳分率
## en
idea: At a set temperature and pressure the feed splits into vapour and liquid in equilibrium. The split is a lever: the feed composition lies between the two phase compositions, and the phase it is closer to is larger. Heat or lower pressure vaporizes more.
eq: V/F = (z − x) / (y − x)
sym: V/F vapour fraction · z, x, y one component's mole fraction in feed, liquid, vapour
