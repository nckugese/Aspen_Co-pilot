## zh
idea: 迴流比越大，塔內上升的蒸汽越多，分離越好，但餾出量固定時，冷凝器與再沸器負荷隨 R + 1 成比例增加。迴流比與板數互相取捨：最小迴流比需要無限多板，設計常取最小值的 1.1 到 1.5 倍。
eq: Q_R ≈ (R + 1)·D·λ
sym: Q_R 再沸器負荷 · R 迴流比 · D 餾出莫耳流量 · λ 莫耳汽化熱（飽和液體進料時的近似）
## en
idea: More reflux sends more vapour up the column and sharpens the split, but at fixed distillate the condenser and reboiler duty grow with R + 1. Reflux trades against stages: minimum reflux needs infinite stages, and designs often take 1.1 to 1.5 times it.
eq: Q_R ≈ (R + 1)·D·λ
sym: Q_R reboiler duty · R reflux ratio · D distillate molar flow · λ molar heat of vaporization (saturated-liquid feed)
