## zh
idea: 用熱物流加熱冷物流，加熱與冷卻的公用流體可同時省下。允許的溫差越小，回收的熱越多，但所需換熱面積越大；最小溫差 ΔTmin 就是這個取捨，常取 10 °C 上下。熱不應跨過夾點傳遞。
eq: Q = U·A·ΔT_lm
sym: Q 熱負荷 · U 總括傳熱係數 · A 換熱面積 · ΔT_lm 對數平均溫差
## en
idea: Heating a cold stream with a hot one saves heating and cooling utility. The closer the temperatures may get, the more heat is recovered and the more area needed; the minimum approach ΔTmin sets the trade, often about 10 °C. No heat should cross the pinch.
eq: Q = U·A·ΔT_lm
sym: Q duty · U overall heat-transfer coefficient · A exchanger area · ΔT_lm log-mean temperature difference
