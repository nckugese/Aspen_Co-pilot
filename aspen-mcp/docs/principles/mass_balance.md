## zh
idea: 穩態時，流進一個單元（或任何框起來的範圍）的質量等於流出的質量。沒有反應時每個成分各自守恆；有反應時只剩總質量與各元素守恆。框可以畫在一台設備、一段流程或整廠。
eq: Σ ṁ_in = Σ ṁ_out
sym: ṁ 質量流量 · in／out 跨過邊界進入與離開的各股物流
## en
idea: At steady state, the mass entering a unit (or any boundary drawn around part of a plant) equals the mass leaving. Without reaction each component balances on its own; with reaction only total mass and each element do.
eq: Σ ṁ_in = Σ ṁ_out
sym: ṁ mass flow · in/out the streams crossing the boundary inward and outward
