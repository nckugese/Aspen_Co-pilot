## zh
idea: 兩個成分 K 值的比，代表蒸餾分開它們有多容易。α 越接近 1 越難分，需要更多板數與更大的迴流；α 等於 1 時汽液組成相同，就是共沸。α 隨溫度與組成改變，壓力升高時通常變小。
eq: α = K_L / K_H
sym: K = y/x 汽液平衡比 · L 較易揮發的成分 · H 較難揮發的成分
## en
idea: Two components' K-value ratio: how easily distillation splits them. The nearer α is to 1, the more stages and reflux it takes; at α = 1 vapour and liquid match: an azeotrope. α shifts with temperature and composition and usually falls as pressure rises.
eq: α = K_L / K_H
sym: K = y/x vapour–liquid ratio · L the more volatile component · H the less volatile one
