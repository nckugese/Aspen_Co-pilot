## zh
idea: 循環把未反應物送回進料，提高整體轉化率；但不反應、也不隨產品離開的成分會在迴路中越積越多，直到排放帶走的量等於進料帶進的量。排放越少，迴路中惰性物越濃，循環流量也越大。
eq: y_I = F·z_I / P
sym: y_I 迴路中惰性物分率 · F 新鮮進料流量 · z_I 進料中惰性物分率 · P 排放流量（惰性物只從排放離開時）
## en
idea: A recycle returns unreacted feed and raises overall conversion, but anything that neither reacts nor leaves with a product builds up in the loop until the purge removes as much as the feed brings in. A smaller purge means more inerts and a larger recycle.
eq: y_I = F·z_I / P
sym: y_I inert fraction in the loop · F fresh feed flow · z_I inert fraction in the feed · P purge flow (inerts leave by the purge only)
